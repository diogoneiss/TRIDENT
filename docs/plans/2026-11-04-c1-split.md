# C1: does the `[NULL]`/`[MASK]` split earn its place? (plan, 2026-11-04)

The protocol is the wiki's `projects/trident/protocol-c1-split.md` (draft of 2026-10-23, owner decisions of
2026-10-23 and 2026-10-24). This plan fixes what the fork runs; each cut gets its own ticket under
[tickets/c1-split/](../tickets/c1-split/) and an experimentation-log entry before its first cell. The user chose C1
after C15 closed ("vamos trabalhar no c1", 2026-11-04 12:10 GMT-3).

## The claim

Per-column `[NULL]` kept apart from per-column `[MASK]` (S, TRIDENT's design) beats one tied token per column (T)
on classification under value-dependent missingness: Δ = macro-F1(S) − macro-F1(T), pooled over the four primary
files, with its plain 95% interval above 0 (Supported), labelled "relevant" at Δ ≥ +0.010 and "real but small"
below it. Reversed is symmetric; Null is an interval inside ±0.010 with both gates passing; anything else is
Inconclusive. Per-file verdicts are flags, never vetoes.

## Decisions

From the protocol (owner, 2026-10-23 and 24), unchanged: decision mode `--cv_folds 5` × seeds 42/7/13 (15 paired
replicates at 70/10/20), short mode seed 42 alone; MNAR primary with the matched MCAR files as the fixed-sequence
second step; zero tuning, one configuration for every arm; the shipped checkpoint rule; readouts written in-run
(per-row probabilities, the kept state); `torch.compile` off; the integrity smoke with deterministic algorithms
forced, or on the CPU if too slow.

Taken by the user on 2026-11-04 (12:40 GMT-3):
- **The pre-training objective:** `embedding_normalized` by explicit override on every arm, as in C15. No ADR, no
  default change, no fixture moves (the protocol's Open decision 6C, an ADR, is not taken). The anchor block passes
  `embedding` explicitly.
- **Open decisions 14a, 14b and 11, as recommended:** the gates G1 (outcome channel) and G2 (pre-training channel)
  condition only the Null row; G2 passes on the interval of S − B excluding 0; "tied by the model" is a median
  cos([MASK]_j, [NULL]_j) ≥ 0.9 after pre-training.
- **Scope now:** cuts 1–3 and the anchor. Cut 4 (rate and dial) follows if the GPUs fall idle, in its own ticket;
  cut 5 (F, M_ft, P4) and the conditional cuts 6a/6b wait on cut 3's reading and their own tickets.
