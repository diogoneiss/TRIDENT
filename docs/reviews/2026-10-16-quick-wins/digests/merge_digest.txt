PROBE setup: The 'ice' field holds ice_seq, not the template's own transform. ice_seq uses the template MiceImputer's fit: MissForestImputer rounds with BayesianRidge for numerical columns and LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000) for categorical ones. That fit matches sklearn IterativeImputer(BayesianRidge) to the last digit. The test rows are then filled the way sklearn's transform does it: the model of round 1, then round 2, and so on up to the kept round, with no stop rule. One difference from sklearn: the number of rounds is the template's still_converging count (3 to 10 per fold), not sklearn's fixed max_iter=10. Matching sklearn on that count gives agreement within 1.4e-9 on biodeg_20nan and kc2_20nan (10 rounds) and within 1e-12 on vehicle_20nan against sklearn(max_iter=6). Running default sklearn on vehicle_20nan differs by up to 1.19, so reproducing these numbers needs the round count set. The template's own fill (MissForestImputer.impute replays the kept round's models on test rows that start at the means) is reported in every row's note as template_replay. It scores 2.0 to 18.8 on the numerical tables (see caveats).

Pre-registered versus post-hoc. The condexp and SoftImpute rules and the 0.003 tie band are frozen in impprobe_arms_v1_preregistered.py (mtime 11:02:27). That predates the first score, impprobe_knncheck.jsonl at 11:02:58.
- condexp: pairwise-complete covariance around the training observed means, turned into a correlation, shrunk as R <- 0.9R + 0.1I, eigenvalues floored at 1e-3. Each test row gets E[x_m | x_o] from its observed numerical cells only, in the frame's scaled space. Categorical columns get the training mode.
- SoftImpute: columns centred on the training means. Rank k is the smallest k whose top eigenvalues of the pairwise covariance reach 90% of its energy, capped at p//2 and p-1. Fit is hard-impute (iterative rank-k SVD) on the training fold: start at 0, at most 100 iterations, tolerance 1e-5. Loadings W = V_k diag(s_k)/sqrt(n). A test row is filled by the PPCA posterior z = (W_o'W_o + s2 I)^-1 W_o'(x_o - mu_o), with s2 the mean of the discarded eigenvalues. Categorical columns get the training mode.
- Added after the seed-42 check on kc2/vehicle and the sklearn cross-check: ice_seq, ice_seq_clip (clipped inside every round to the column's training [min, max], i.e. sklearn's min_value/max_value), condexp_clip and softimpute_clip (clipped at the end). NOT QUICK:
 - ReMasker (clean-room fork-native) : No LICENSE file, so it can only be reimplemented clean-room (17-24 h). The stock code parses sys.argv, calls sys.exit(1) on a non-finite loss, and never seeds. Training hits a 0/0 loss, near-certain on credit-g_80nan, kr-vs-kp_60/80nan, spambase_60/80nan, vehicle_60nan and biodeg_60nan. It is numeric-only. 15-25 GPU-h at 300 epochs for 63 (variant, seed) pairs off-store, or about 40 GPU-h per BACKLOG I3. Do it after MaCoDE; the BACKLOG I3 design is ready.
 - HyperImpute package (AutoML imputer, GAIN, MIWAE, Sinkhorn) : 0.1.17, dormant since 2023:
- the AutoML imputer crashes on sklearn 1.9 (LogisticRegression multi_class);
- the GAIN and SoftImpute plugins silently ignore their hyperparameters (issue #42; plugin_softimpute.py:298);
- MIWAE defaults to a 1-unit network and crashes on a zero scale (#44);
- HyperImpute, Sinkhorn and EM are transductive;
- every plugin reseeds the global numpy, torch and random streams.
The AutoML imputer would cost 10-60 CPU-h. Its linear plugins are already reproduced in numpy by probe D.
 - TabImpute zero-shot : Pins tabpfn==6.0.0, which needs sklearn<1.8 against the fork's 1.9: a separate venv plus a subprocess bridge. No code licence. Transductive in context, and the paper's setting reads the global RNG. Only 6 of 21 variants (kc2, vehicle, biodeg) are in its validated regime. Categorical support is unvalidated and its categorical scripts are broken. 12-20 h. No published MNAR specialist weights.
 - TabPFN extension imputer (tabpfn-extensions TabPFNUnsupervisedModel.impute) : Cost is quadratic in the number of columns: about 7.6M TabPFN calls, roughly 1,000 GPU-h, or about 100 in fast mode. The output is a stochastic sample. It pulls in telemetry and has no device handling. Use the column-wise subclass (item 12) instead.
 - not-MIWAE, GINA, NIMIWAE, Heckman imputer : All are blocked on item 7's MNAR family: on today's MCAR holes a selection model is only a misspecification control.
- not-MIWAE: the MIT PyTorch port lacks multi-class categorical heads and needs a real-vs-artificial mask split. 18-28 h, 20-50 device-h.
- GINA: archived 2020-era code. It needs fully observed auxiliary columns, which the fork's all-column injector never leaves.
- NIMIWAE: GPL-3, R/reticulate, hard-coded CUDA.
- Heckman: 12-20 h, fragile identification under self-masking.
Delta-adjusted hgb (item 10) gives most of the cheap insight.
 - CACTI/CMAE and DiffPuter : CACTI: GPL-3, CLI-only, a separate conda environment and a language-model download. Under MCAR its copy masking reduces to ReMasker's random masking. Its idea (CMAE) costs 3-5 h inside a ReMasker reimplementation once MNAR exists.
DiffPuter: a dataset-specific script, about 1,000-2,500 s per fit (100-200 GPU-h for the grid), with NaN and non-convergence failures on small tables. At most a later 15-30 GPU-h slice on the three large 20nan tables.
 - Distributional metrics (sliced Wasserstein, energy distance) on today's MCAR default : They need all 5 fold ledgers. MLflow keeps only the best and worst folds (a biased subset). The rest sit in the server queues' $STATE/results directories, which no sync pulls and which may have been cleaned. Current-default ledgers cover 7 of 21 variants, and baseline fills are never persisted. Feasible path: compute them on item 7's laptop cells, whose ledgers are all local, or after the deterministic rerun below.
 - Deterministic rerun of the 63 default cells with --save_model and train/validation ledgers : A TRIDENT retrain (about 7-8 h on gorgona8) plus a hook in decoding.py. It unlocks true impute-then-learn (Le Morvan & Varoquaux), full-coverage ledgers and the cosine probe. Worth doing; it pairs naturally with item 5's fresh-sweep alternative. Medium, not quick.
 - TRIDENT classification at 5 folds x 3 seeds, and the validation-ranked retune (step zero proper) : The 5-fold, 3-seed run is cheap in code (1-2 h; clone gap_token_run.py without decode_gap_token, as config.py:276-281 rejects it for classification) but costs a server night. The validation retune needs a change to opt.py: classification ranks trials on test macro-F1 (types.py:138, opt.py:175-179), and finetuning.py never returns validation F1. It also needs an ADR and tens of GPU-hours. Secondary to imputation; run it on otherwise idle server nights only.
 - t2 tied-token arms and the Table 4 imputation-variant arm for classification : Both touch the protected classification path. Fine-tuning always shows [NULL] (finetuning.py:81-88, 122-124). A default-off flag plus plumbing is about 60 lines (commit d0744ad precedent), half a day to a day, and it needs a classification reference first.
 - Equal-budget HPO (TabZilla-style, 20 trials) and deep classification baselines (FT-Transformer, TabNet, native SAINT, NAIM) : The GBDT half of the HPO is quick (2-3 h), but the TRIDENT half needs the opt.py change. The deep baselines need the 6-10 h harness plus 3-16 h each. All are classification and secondary under the imputation focus.
 - miceforest : With mean matching off it is essentially ADR 0016's withdrawn missforest_lgbm; with it on, it is stochastic. It is a new dependency. Keep it in reserve as a multiple-imputation generator for the classification side.
